**Existing-family lookup:**

Before recording a new finding or actionable family, inspect the families recorded in Invariant Register Carry-forward, Actionable Families, and Finding Dispositions or Prior Finding Dispositions in review-resolution.md and its history under the Report Directory. Also inspect Problem-Family Completion Sweep; Current Iteration Findings (new) or New Findings (new); Carry-over Findings (persists) or Persisting Findings (persists); Resolved Findings (resolved) or Resolved (resolved); and Reopened Findings (reopened) in the latest review reports. When the identity above matches, reuse the existing family. In a review report, preserve that family identity while recording the responsible source, invariant, reason to change from the same cause, and new path in Problem-Family Completion Sweep, and reuse the existing `family_tag` in Current Iteration Findings (new) or New Findings (new). In review-resolution.md or supervisor-validation.md, add the finding ID, source, and affected contract path to the existing Actionable Families row whose Authorization Basis matches that finding. When no row exists for that basis, create another row repeating the same family name. Never combine findings with different bases in one row or overwrite an existing row's basis. In Finding Dispositions or Prior Finding Dispositions, record the target family, the finding's own basis, and the merge rationale. Create a new `family_tag` or family only when no recorded family matches and this role has authority to create a new family.
