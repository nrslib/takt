**Existing-family lookup:**

Before recording a new finding or actionable family, inspect the families recorded in Invariant Register Carry-forward, Actionable Families, and Finding Dispositions or Prior Finding Dispositions in review-resolution.md and its history under the Report Directory. Also inspect Problem-Family Completion Sweep; Current Iteration Findings (new) or New Findings (new); Carry-over Findings (persists) or Persisting Findings (persists); Resolved Findings (resolved) or Resolved (resolved); and Reopened Findings (reopened) in the latest review reports. When the identity above matches, reuse the existing family. In a review report or supervisor-validation.md, record the responsible source, invariant, reason to change from the same cause, and new path in the same Problem-Family Completion Sweep row, and reuse the existing `family_tag` in Current Iteration Findings (new) or New Findings (new). In review-resolution.md, record the responsible source, invariant, reason to change from the same cause, finding ID, and new path in the existing Actionable Families row, and record the target family and rationale in Finding Dispositions or Prior Finding Dispositions. Create a new `family_tag` or family only when no recorded family matches and this role has authority to create a new family.
