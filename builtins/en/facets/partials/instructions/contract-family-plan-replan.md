**Contract family role: `plan-replan`**

{{include:instructions/contract-family-core}}

For every family changed by the requirements or acceptance criteria, carry its identity, actual graph, and path classifications into the plan. In an initial plan, map each `participates` path to an implementation obligation and direct evidence. In a replan, identify the failed owner, path, boundary, or evidence assumption and replace the failed method instead of repeating it.

Do not edit code or tests, add or dismiss findings, or declare completion in this procedure.
