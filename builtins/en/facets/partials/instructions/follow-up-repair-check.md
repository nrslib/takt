After a repair, compare every accepted finding and fix-plan completion criterion with the current code, the supplied `fix-plan.md` and `fix-report.md`, and required test evidence. These report inputs are bound to the completed remediation step for this review. If required evidence is missing, identify it. Check affected consumers, obsolete paths, preserved contracts, and tests required by the changed behavior. Treat recorded test results as evidence only for exercised conditions, and identify unverified conditions and remaining concrete defects.
