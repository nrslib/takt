For a follow-up finding, `Authorization Basis` must be exactly one value selected under the applicable policy from `accepted_family_unvisited_consumer`, `direct_acceptance_criterion_violation`, `remediation_regression`, or `required_consumer_migration`; combined values and every other value are invalid. Initial review uses `not applicable`. `Reason Absent from Initial Round` is a separate factual explanation for a follow-up finding and is `not applicable` for initial review.
